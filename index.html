A little note before we begin

Two years,
one little book.

I don't really know how to fit two years into a website.

There are too many pictures, too many stupid jokes, too many places we've been, too many things I've forgotten and then remembered because of a random photo sitting in my camera roll.

So I decided to put some of it here instead.

This is our little scrapbook. Not necessarily the biggest moments, or even the most important ones, just little pieces of the last two years that made us, us.

From the first date, to all the coffees, birthdays, stupid pictures, random adventures, promises, laughs, and everything in between.

Two years of memories, all in one place.

So, I guess we should start where it all began.

— us, probably

And I still don't think
I managed to fit two years in here.

There are so many things missing. Conversations I don't have screenshots of. Days that don't have pictures. Moments that only the two of us remember. All the completely ordinary days that somehow became some of my favourites.

But maybe that's okay.

Because these pictures aren't really the two years. They're just little pieces of it.

The real two years were in the laughs, the stupid jokes, the long conversations, the random plans, the arguments, the making up, the places we kept going back to, and all the tiny moments in between.

And if I could go back and relive any of it, I think I'd choose all of it.

The big moments.

The stupid ones.

The ordinary ones.

You.

Us.

Two years down.

And hopefully, a whole lot more to go.

Happy two years, Sweetie.
I love you.
